How to Play Tank Battle

How to Play Helicopter Strike

How to Play Bike Stunt Rider

How to Play — Tesla Flying Car

Modes: Stunt Run (start lights, ramp, rings, flips, landing rules), Free Drive/Fly (open drive and flight, LAND whenever you like) and Racing (beat the ghost rival to the finish). Keys: W/↑ accelerate, S/↓ brake, A/D or ←/→ steer and roll, ↑/↓ pitch in the air, Space = thrusters, F = FLY, L = LAND (auto-landing — always lands successfully), Shift = nitro, C = camera. Start lights: pressing DRIVE or Space before green shows “Too early, wait for green”, plays a beep and locks input for 0.4s — it never launches. On green, a faster reaction means a faster ramp speed (76–90 mph). Rings are worth +500 and refill thruster gas. A manual landing succeeds only if sink speed ≤ 39 ft/s and tilt ≤ 28°; otherwise the car crashes. Score = rings × 500 + flips × 1500 + altitude + airtime + soft-landing bonus.
